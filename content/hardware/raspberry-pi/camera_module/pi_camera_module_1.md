The original Raspberry Pi Camera Module 1 (v1.3) features a 5-megapixel OmniVision OV5647 fixed-focus sensor. 
<!--more-->
It supports 2592 × 1944 still images and video recording at 1080p30, 720p60, and 640x480p90 via a 15-pin MIPI CSI ribbon cable. 
The board measures 25 × 20 × 9 mm and weighs roughly 3 grams.

## Technical Specifications

* Sensor: OmniVision OV5647 (color CMOS, fixed focus)
* Resolution: 5 Megapixels
* Max Still Photo Resolution: 2592 × 1944 pixels
* Video Modes: 1080p at 30fps, 720p at 60fps, and 640 × 480p at 60/90fps
* Interface: 15-pin MIPI Camera Serial Interface (CSI) ribbon cable
* Dimensions: 25 mm × 20 mm × 9 mm
* Weight: 3g to 9g (depending on exact casing/cable batch variant)Variants: 
* Standard (visible light) and NoIR (infrared sensitive, no IR filter)
